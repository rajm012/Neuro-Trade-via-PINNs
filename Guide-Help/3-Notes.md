# Nifty 50: NIFTY 50 is a benchmark stock market index that represents the performance of the 50 largest and most actively traded companies on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India, spanning diverse sectors of the economy.

    - Top 50 stock may change by various factor like valuation, controversy and so.
